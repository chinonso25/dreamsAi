Remember your dreams before the details fade. The Dreamer is a dream journal for writing a few words, recording your voice, and returning to the moments you want to keep.

CAPTURE IN YOUR OWN WAY
Write a quick note or record a voice entry while the dream is still fresh. Text and recordings save on your device first, so you can capture a dream without waiting for a connection.

TURN VOICE INTO TEXT
When you choose, turn a saved recording into a readable transcript. Your original audio stays available to replay.

MAKE ENTRIES EASIER TO REVISIT
Request an AI summary, title, mood, and tags for an entry. Review and edit the result alongside your own words. Summaries help organise what you recorded.

FIND THE DREAM YOU REMEMBER
Search your entries by words and themes, browse by date, and keep favourites close. Revisit familiar people, places, and feelings in your own journal.

BUILD YOUR MORNING ROUTINE
Set a journaling reminder that suits you. Start with a fragment, a feeling, or a few spoken words; each entry adds to the story you can return to.

KEEP A COPY OF YOUR WORDS
Edit your entries, share a dream when you want to, or export a readable copy of your journal. Optional email recovery helps you reconnect with your account.

AI transcription and summaries use cloud processing when you request them. Premium subscriptions provide additional AI processing. Your saved journal remains available when a subscription ends.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.
