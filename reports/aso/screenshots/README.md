# Screenshot plan for The Dreamer

Prepared for en-US, en-GB, en-CA and en-AU. `headings.json` contains the editable copy, in order. Use the same six images for the four English locales, with favorite/favourite spelling adapted on frame 5.

The current 1.0.4 draft contains four inherited images in APP_IPHONE_67 at 1320 × 2868. No screenshot set was replaced in this ASO task. The app does not declare tablet support, so an iPad campaign is outside this set.

| Order | Heading | Real app screen to capture | Supporting line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Keep your dreams before they fade | Journal home with several representative demo entries | A journal for your words and voice. |
| 2 | Speak it now. Read it later. | Saved recording entry with playback and a completed transcript | Record first. Choose transcription later. |
| 3 | Revisit a dream with AI summaries | Detail view with summary, original words, mood and tags visible | Your words, with a summary you can review. |
| 4 | Find the words and themes you kept | Search results for a recognisable theme with the date controls visible | Search words, themes, and dates. |
| 5 | Keep your favourite dreams | Journal with the favourites filter active and multiple saved entries | Return to the moments you want to keep. |
| 6 | Make room for a morning ritual | Settings with the actual reminder control visible | A reminder for your journaling routine. |

Lead with capture, voice and summaries in the first three images; these communicate the actual product quickly. Avoid interpretation, lucid-dream training, sleep monitoring, automatic cross-entry analysis, improved sleep, guaranteed recall, or a completely offline AI promise.

Use the app's existing lavender palette (#FAF8FF paper, #2A2243 ink, #7058CE accent), with ink headings on light backgrounds and white headings on dark backgrounds. Prefer one clear benefit per frame. Preserve the actual UI rather than drawing fictional screens. Use synthetic dream entries created specifically for marketing; inspect transcripts and summaries for coherent content.

Existing reports/rebuild captures include desktop simulator controls, cursor overlays and an older journal layout. They are unsuitable as final screenshot inputs. A simulator was booted during this task, but it was at the Home screen; no fresh app screen was verified. Do not treat that as screenshot completion.

To render with the invoked Vibe ASO skill, use clean device captures in mockups exported with the heading hidden, named iphone_1.png through iphone_6.png. Keep the top heading band clear. The skill's default iPhone output is 1284 × 2778; configure its renderer for 1320 × 2868 if retaining the current draft size. Choose the final mockups, heading order and odd/even font colours before rendering and visually review every frame. A reusable Figma template is available at https://www.figma.com/design/ftvaN3ZMfgkGrn7SAuqCvt/vibe-ASO-mockups.

No rendered screenshot, upload, or screenshot conversion test is claimed here.
